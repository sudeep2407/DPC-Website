Who We Are

Darjeeling Prime Cab Services is a trusted local taxi operator based in the heart of the Queen of Hills. We specialize in safe, affordable and professional cab services across Darjeeling, Kalimpong, Dooars and the wider North Bengal region.

Our mission is simple: make every journey through the hills as comfortable and worry-free as possible. Whether you are a tourist catching the Tiger Hill sunrise, a family heading to Dooars, or a corporate traveler needing punctual airport transfers — we are here for you.
